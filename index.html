Welcome to the Official Home of Rattles of Shiloh

We are a dedicated church dance team using our God-given talents to minister, inspire, and praise the Almighty here at Kenyatta Road. It is our ultimate mission to minister, inspire, and lift up praise to God in this place.

As a creative dance ministry, we use our diverse talents to create impactful opportunities for community outreach, drawing hearts closer to God. We are blessed to have a wonderful fellowship that inspires many and sounds a loud rumble of praise to the Almighty at Kenyatta Road.

✨ Our Mission

To glorify God through creative and spiritual dance expressions. The Rattles dance team aims to touch hearts, uplift spirits, and lead the congregation into a deep atmosphere of worship during services and special church events.
